This file is the complete text of the film, in order, with timings. It exists
because a rendered video is opaque to search engines, to screen readers, and to
anyone whose player will not autoplay. Nothing in the film is conveyed by
motion, colour or sound alone; everything it says is written below. Paste this
into the body of any post the film is attached to.

THE FILM CARRIES A SILENT AUDIO TRACK. Remotion inserts one by default and it
is kept deliberately, because several platforms reject or mis-handle an MP4
with no audio stream at all. It contains no speech, no music and no sound
effect, so there is nothing for captions to caption. That is a property worth
stating rather than eliding: it means this transcript is complete rather than
supplementary.

ACCESSIBILITY NOTES

- No audio. Nothing requires captions.
- A still contact sheet carrying every word of the film, one frame per act, is
  published beside it as contact-sheet.png. Hand that to anyone who has motion
  reduced at the OS level; a video cannot answer prefers-reduced-motion and
  this is the replacement rather than an apology for it.
- Wherever this film is embedded on a surface its author controls, it must be
  <video controls preload="none" poster="poster.png"> with NO autoplay and NO
  loop. On LinkedIn, autoplay is the platform's behaviour and cannot be
  overridden; that is a known and unfixable regression of this format.
- Every colour pair in the film is measured in motion-lab/tools/contrast.mjs
  against the opaque surface it is drawn on. Lowest measured pair: 3.73:1
  (--text-mute chrome at 30px). Every pair carrying content: >= 5.27:1.
